TEST MASTER — VERY SIMPLE USE

1. Open index.html in Chrome to test the app immediately.
2. Inside the app press “MCQs”.
3. Press “Add One MCQ” to enter questions one by one.
4. Or press “Download MCQ Template”, fill the CSV file, then “Import MCQs File”.
5. Go to “Test” and choose Subject, Chapter, number of questions and time.
6. Take the test. Results are saved on the device.

ANDROID:
The same app is included inside the app/ folder as an Android Studio project. It can be opened in Android Studio and built into APK/AAB.

IMPORTANT:
This package is the full functional v1 app prototype. A signed Play Store AAB still requires a local Android build/signing step and the developer’s Google Play Console account.
